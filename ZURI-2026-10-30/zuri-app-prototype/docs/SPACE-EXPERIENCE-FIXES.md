# Space experience fixes

21 October 2026.

Space activity now uses one shared selector for sample and locally created posts. Hidden posts, blocked/muted authors, restricted Society associations and chapter-only media are excluded before the Space/Society association is evaluated. New posts tagged with a Space appear immediately in its overview and activity list.

The default Space section is Overview, followed by Offerings, Activity, Events, Societies and About. Previously remembered Pulse views map to Activity. The overview prioritises key offerings, linked gatherings and visible recent posts. Existing section/back-state preferences remain respected.

Space search includes offering names and descriptions, including local active offerings. Searching wedding can therefore discover a Space whose business name does not contain that word.

Space and catalogue offering actions now reach the same offering detail, retaining sample price labels such as per-day pricing. Unpriced offerings lead directly to a contextual enquiry. Enquiries keep the Space, selected offering, preferred date, people/quantity and message. They save as explicitly unsent local drafts and never invent a merchant recipient or route to self. Invalid dates, quantities and missing contexts fail safely.

Follow updates, Update preferences, Enquire and Save are separate labelled intentions. Echo preferences remain available, but business enquiries use the shared enquiry flow. Society member actions no longer fall back to an unrelated person's chat.

Automated checks cover hidden/blocked activity, new tagged posts, chapter-only media exclusion, search relevance, enquiry context and saved-data restoration. Browser access was denied earlier in this session; these changes have not received a fresh interactive visual check. No external messages, bookings or payments were made.
