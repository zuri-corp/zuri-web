# Digital presence audit and implementation

Visitor profiles previously showed biography and a raw photo grid. That confused private collection with chosen distribution and offered no useful representation of a person’s work.

Implemented: clear identity and biography; follow/message; channels; curated capabilities/interests; collaboration availability; portfolio links; explicitly featured owned Spaces and their active offerings. Empty sections do not invent credentials or relationships. Self-described capabilities are labelled as such. Raw moments and private Chapters are excluded. Personal presence can be edited and previewed from Lore Profile. Chat labels are context-neutral; routing continues to reuse existing conversations rather than duplicate threads.

Remaining data/service gaps: other people cannot author their presence in this device-only prototype, so only their existing bios and channels are shown as available information. Verified skills, evidence, professional roles, third-party references, meaningful public contact rules, booking availability, endorsements and event-host relationships require explicit structured records and authorization. No XP or sample scores are presented as trust credentials. No employment, affiliation, skill or service ownership is inferred from a tagged photo or a bio.

Product direction: visitor profiles should answer who this person is, what they create, what they can do, what they offer, where they participate and how to approach them. Show user-selected public evidence and activity through channels, not an automatic dump of their private collection. Keep account settings and financial history on the owner side.
