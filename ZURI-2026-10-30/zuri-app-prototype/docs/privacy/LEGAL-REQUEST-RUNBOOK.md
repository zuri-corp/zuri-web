# Legal requests and disclosure — proposed operating procedure

This is an engineering/operations proposal for qualified counsel to approve, not a legal opinion or a promise to users. Ugandan law and every launch/hosting jurisdiction require assessment. Reference: official Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, https://fia.go.ug/sites/default/files/downloads/Data-Protection-and-Privacy-Act-2019.pdf . Do not assume the statute overrides other valid disclosure/preservation obligations.

1. Receive demands through a designated legal channel. Verify requestor identity independently and preserve the demand in a restricted case system. Do not give routine support agents raw export access.
2. Counsel establishes authority, jurisdiction, required process, scope, deadlines, confidentiality, preservation duty and available grounds to challenge or narrow. Assess rights risks and sensitive-community impacts without logging sensitive-group labels on user profiles.
3. Preserve only the records legally required under the validated scope, with approved access, expiry/review dates and a documented chain of custody. Do not evade or destroy evidence in response to a demand. Do not treat every informal request as a broad hold.
4. Identify data actually held by each controller/processor. Distinguish readable records, ciphertext, keys not possessed and data never collected. Do not claim technical impossibility without verifying it.
5. Notify the user in advance where lawful and feasible; record the legal basis for any prohibition or delay and review it. Do not promise notification in every case.
6. Challenge unlawful/overbroad requests where available; require proper process for scope changes. Use a documented two-person legal/authorized disclosure approval.
7. Produce the minimum required records via a secure verified destination. Restrict transfer access and record what was produced; do not expose unrelated people or whole workspaces unnecessarily.
8. Track case review/closure, release of preservation obligations, retention and deletion. User access, correction and deletion workflows must respect applicable legal exceptions with a clear explanation.
9. Publish legally permitted aggregate demand/disclosure/challenge statistics and relevant policy changes without identifying affected users or violating restrictions.

Emergency requests require a defined counsel-reviewed escalation path and recorded reasoning, not automatic bulk access. Staff coercion, account compromise and demands for future interception require separate senior legal/security review and incident planning.

Production prerequisites: verified legal ownership, jurisdiction analysis, registration/obligations assessment, processor contracts, secure case system, staff training, audited disclosure tooling and a tabletop rehearsal. None is implemented by adding a privacy settings screen.
