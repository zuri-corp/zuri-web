## Icons and brand references

Public site icons were obtained from providers’ own websites and their linked asset hosts, saved locally without recolouring or redrawing. Some are corporate icons identifying the provider rather than a separate product-suite wordmark. The local source manifest is public/brands/tools/manifest.json. This source manifest documents origin, not a universal trademark licence.

23 official-source icons are displayed. QuickBooks and Adobe Express use neutral text references with generic arrow tiles instead of their restricted logos:

- [Intuit trademark policy](https://www.intuit.com/legal/trademark/): plain-text descriptive references permitted; product logos require a written licence.
- [Adobe trademark policy](https://www.adobe.com/legal/permissions/trademarks.html): logo and product-icon use generally requires a licence.
- [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/): unmodified marks, suitable spacing, direct product reference and no implied endorsement.
- [Canva trademark policy](https://www.canva.com/policies/trademark-policy/).
- [Notion trademark guidelines](https://notion.notion.site/Notion-Trademark-Usage-Guidelines-9826313c686a4f6e9d8a48347162714b).

No provider permission requests, memberships or agreements were submitted. Unused downloaded Adobe favicon is not referenced in the UI. Permission checks and brand rules remain provider-specific.

## Behaviour

Category selectors show five tools per category. Search matches names, task descriptions and category words. Empty results can reset to all tools. Setup links carry allowlisted product IDs, service, goal and audience into the existing reviewable enquiry form. Existing Notion, Google Workspace, Canva and QuickBooks product links retain their IDs.
