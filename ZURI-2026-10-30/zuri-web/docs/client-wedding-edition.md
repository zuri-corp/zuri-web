# Client wedding design edition

Reference inspected: https://www.gideonwedsmargie.com/ on 15 October 2026. User confirmed the couple are clients and authorized reuse of their photographs in ZURI Invites.

All four template treatments now feature Gideon and Margie using their actual public wedding date and venue. They are labelled as alternative design previews, rather than official replacement invitations. Six photo URLs from the proposal and Kukyaala collections are referenced directly from the client site. Their availability therefore depends on that site retaining the assets.

New features: Kampala-time countdown with a zero floor after the ceremony, eight-step timeline, venue directions, six-image gallery with an accessible native dialog viewer, FAQs and a fuller guest-response preview. Native dialog Escape, next/previous controls and focus return are supported. Preview forms never submit responses or collect guest information remotely. No payment details, pledge form, private contacts or WhatsApp group invitation were copied into the templates. The existing couple’s live wedding website remains the source for actual guest responses and current details.

The Garden uses a botanical portrait composition; After Dark uses a cinematic full-bleed photograph; Under the Sun uses warm contemporary typography; A Quiet Promise uses a restrained ivory composition. The collection now shows real client photography on every design card.
