# Living Spaces demonstration — 25 October 2026

Pamoja now makes the entrances explicit: the ZURI app for people, reviewed Space setup for establishments. The business section leads with three outcomes: be understood, keep the conversation moving, and bring people together. Detailed capabilities remain expandable. Spaces complement an existing website; they are no longer positioned as a substitute for businesses unable to afford one.

The reusable Space tour opens on the fictional Common Ground café and its **Life here** view. Four contributions demonstrate a customer moment, a creator story, a gathering proposal and an official business update. The studio offers a parallel example. Visitors can filter contribution types, open a story, inspect authorship and prepare an enquiry about the linked offering. Changing establishment resets story selection and enquiry state. Review never sends a message, books a service or publishes content.

Oliver Reed and Kai Morgan link to the existing explicitly fictional contributor profiles. Images are illustrative stock photographs, not evidence of real visits or endorsements. No follower counts, reach, attendance or results are invented. Authorship and business updates remain distinct; linking a Space does not transfer advertising rights.

Existing crawlable public profile pages, real-profile structured data and sitemap rules are retained. Fictional profiles and the tour remain excluded from claims of real structured business presence. Production indexing depends on the configured canonical origin and live publication.

Pending product work remains live content delivery, contributor/owner permissions, moderation and reporting, featuring requests and rights records, account persistence and real enquiry handling. The website demonstrates the proposition rather than supplying a live social network.
